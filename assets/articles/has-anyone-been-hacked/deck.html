The short answer

  • No organisation has said it was breached through php2shell, in its own words or a regulator's, as of 12:14 UTC on 26 October.
  • Attempts are certain and massive: millions at Cloudflare; Amazon's decoy server hit on 17 October, four days before the fix.
  • Vendors say "exploited in the wild", but name no victim: Hadrian, Patchstack, Amazon (against its own sensors), NCSC New Zealand.
  • Not on CISA's Known Exploited list at the last check (24 October); CISA's own CVE record says "Exploitation: none".
  • Hundreds of sites went offline as a precaution. Offline is a decision, not a breach.

What would change the answer

  1. A named organisation saying it was compromised through php2shell, in its own notice, filing or statement.
  2. GMO, Czech Technical University or DMM stating that php2shell was the cause.
  3. A CISA KEV listing for either CVE, with CISA's evidence.
  4. A regulator's breach notice naming the flaw.
  5. A security vendor naming a victim, with the victim's consent.
